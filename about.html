Built for people who work with words.

TextTools is a small collection of text utilities made to be genuinely useful — not upsold, not logged, not complicated.

Where it came from

TextTools is part of Atom_Wrek — an independent initiative building free, practical, browser-based tools for everyday users. The idea is simple: the tools people keep searching for should just exist, work immediately, and ask nothing in return.

No accounts. No uploads to a server. No paywalls on the features that actually matter. Everything runs locally in your browser, which means your text never leaves your device.

What we stand by

Private by designYour text stays in your browser. Always.
No account neededOpen and use. Nothing more required.
Free, fullyNo plans, no tiers, no upgrade walls.
Always growingNew tools added as they're ready.

The tools so far

We started with the four we found ourselves needing most: a word and character counter, a case converter, a duplicate line remover, and a whitespace cleaner. More are in the works — built the same way, with the same principles.
