Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 March 2026

Short version: TextTools does not collect, store, or transmit your text or any personal data. Everything runs entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

1. Who we are

TextTools is a project by Atom_Wrek, an independent initiative building free browser-based tools. You can reach us at atom-wrek@proton.me.

2. What data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. We do not use analytics. We do not log your text, your IP address, or any information about how you use the tools.

All tool processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. No text or file content is ever sent to a server.

3. Google Fonts

TextTools loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may receive your IP address and browser user agent as part of the standard HTTP request. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Hosting

TextTools is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard server-side access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) as part of its infrastructure. This data is governed by Vercel's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to or control over this data.

5. Google AdSense

TextTools may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information, see Google's advertising policies.

6. Cookies

TextTools itself does not set any cookies. Any cookies present on this site originate from third-party services such as Google AdSense or Google Fonts, as described above.

7. GDPR — EEA users

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we do not collect or store any personal data directly, there is nothing for us to provide or delete on our end. For data processed by third-party services (Google Fonts, Vercel, Google AdSense), please refer to their respective privacy policies and contact them directly.

To exercise your rights or ask a question, contact us at atom-wrek@proton.me.

8. CCPA — California users

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because TextTools does not collect, sell, or share personal information, most CCPA provisions do not apply to our direct operations. We do not sell personal data to third parties. For data handled by third-party services, please review their privacy policies.

For any CCPA-related requests, contact us at atom-wrek@proton.me.

9. Children's privacy

TextTools is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our site, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of TextTools after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For any questions, concerns, or data requests relating to this privacy policy, please email atom-wrek@proton.me.
